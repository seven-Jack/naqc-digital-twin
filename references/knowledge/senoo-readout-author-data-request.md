Dear Professor Kaufman,

I am building a digital twin for a ¹⁷¹Yb neutral-atom platform and using your paper (DOI 10.1038/s41567-026-03258-8) as a reference for dual-exposure spin and loss readout. I have reproduced the paired-count comparison from the published Fig. 2b source workbook. Could you share the underlying per-attempt records for Fig. 2b and Extended Data Table 1 in any format convenient for your group?

A table of paired ROI counts would be sufficient for an initial comparison. The most useful fields are run, attempt, site and frame identifiers, the preparation label (m₁, m₀ or empty), the trigger and exposure times for both images, the applied count thresholds and decision rules, and the preparation-erasure or other exclusion flags. Records for excluded attempts would let me reconstruct each denominator. A short note on ROI and background processing would help preserve the original count convention.

If an independently acquired batch from the same setup and readout settings already exists, its run identifier and matching records would let me test the fixed decision rule. If the per-attempt records are difficult to share, the cell counts and denominators behind Extended Data Table 1, together with the threshold and selection rules, the treatment of double-bright results, and the uncertainty calculation, would also be valuable.

Thank you for your time and for making the figure source data available.
